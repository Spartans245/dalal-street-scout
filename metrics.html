Fundamentals

Balance sheet and valuation metrics. PE and MCap from NSE. D/E and ROE pending data source decision.

MetricSourceRaw Data RequiredFormula / Notes
PE Ratio ✅ NSE PE Ratio Sourced directly from NSE quote-equity (pdSymbolPe) — all 2,736 stocks
Sector Median PE ✅ CALC PE of all stocks in sector median(PE) grouped by sector — excludes stocks with PE ≤ 0 (no earnings) — computed post-scan in enrich_sector_pe()
PE vs Sector ✅ CALC Stock PE  ·  Sector Median PE (stock PE − sector median PE) ÷ sector median PE × 100
F score tiers: 0 to +20% → 12pts (sweet spot)  /  −20 to 0% → 10pts  /  −40 to −20% → 7pts  /  +20 to +40% → 5pts  /  >+40% → 2pts  /  −60 to −40% → 2pts  /  <−60% → 0pts (value trap)
D/E Ratio ⏸ PENDING YF D/E Ratio Not available from Kite or NSE. Yahoo Finance covers ~1,400 of 2,750 stocks. Decision: drop from F score or find full-coverage source.
ROE ⏸ PENDING YF ROE Not available from Kite or NSE. Yahoo Finance covers ~1,400 stocks. Display only — not scored. Decide together with D/E.
MCap ✅ NSE MCap Sourced directly from NSE trade_info (totalMarketCap) — all 2,750 stocks. Used for MCap filter tabs and tiebreak sort.

Technical

All technical metrics computed from Kite OHLCV history (193 trading days ~9.5 months). Shared math in shared/technicals.py.

MetricSourceRaw Data RequiredFormula
RSI (14) ✅ KITE Daily Close (14+ days) gain = Wilder smooth of positive daily changes (α=1/14); loss = Wilder smooth of negative daily changes (α=1/14); RSI = 100 − 100/(1 + gain/loss)
EMA 14 ✅ KITE Daily Close (14+ days) Exponential moving average, span = 14
EMA 50 ✅ KITE Daily Close (50+ days) Exponential moving average, span = 50
EMA Cross ✅ KITE Daily Close (50+ days) EMA14 crosses above EMA50 — detected within last 5 days
EMA Pre-Cross ✅ KITE Daily Close (50+ days) EMA14 below EMA50, gap < 0.5% of price, EMA14 rising faster than 3 days ago
EMA Post-Cross ✅ KITE Daily Close (50+ days) Cross happened 1–2 days ago AND EMA14–EMA50 gap still < 1.5% of price
EMA Pullback ✅ KITE Daily Close (50+ days) Cross happened ≥ 2 days ago AND price was > 2% above EMA14 in last 5 days (confirmed move away) AND price is now 0–2% above EMA14 (dipped back, still above)
EMA Trend ✅ KITE Daily Close (50+ days) EMA14 > EMA50, no fresh cross in last 5 days
MACD Signal ✅ KITE Daily Close (26+ days) MACD line = EMA(12) − EMA(26); Signal = EMA(MACD, 9); True if MACD crossed above Signal today
Golden Cross ✅ KITE Daily Close (190+ days) EMA30 > EMA190
ADX (14) ✅ KITE Daily High, Low, Close (28+ days) TR = max(H−L, |H−Cprev|, |L−Cprev|); +DM / −DM per bar; Wilder smooth(14) → ATR, +DI, −DI; DX = 100 × |+DI−−DI| / (+DI+−DI); ADX = Wilder smooth(DX, 14)
VPB — Price Coiling ✅ KITE Daily Close (6 days) (max − min of last 5 closes) / min < 0.04 (range < 4%)
VPB — Vol Shrinking ✅ KITE Daily Volume (25 days) All of last 3 days volume < 85% of 20-day avg baseline
VPB — Vol Ratio ✅ KITE Daily Volume (25 days) Today volume ÷ avg20 baseline
VPB — Close Position ✅ KITE Daily High, Low, Close (Close − Low) ÷ (High − Low)
VPB Score ✅ KITE Daily High, Low, Close, Volume (25+ days) BRK✓ +10 (setup + vol≥2× + pos≥0.70)  /  BRK✓ +7 (setup + vol≥1.5× + pos≥0.60)  /  BRK~ +5 (setup + vol≥1.5× + pos<0.60)  /  VOL +4 (no setup + vol≥2× + pos≥0.70)  /  COIL✓ +3 (setup + vol<1×)  /  COIL~ +2 (price coiling only)  /  DIST −2 (setup + vol≥1.5× + pos<0.30)
Vol Confirmed Cross ✅ KITE Daily Volume, Close (50+ days) Cross day volume ≥ 1.5× 20-day avg before cross
Cross Score ✅ KITE Daily Volume, Close (50+ days) 18 (vol confirmed, 1–2d ago)  /  14 (vol confirmed, 3–4d ago)  /  10 (vol confirmed, 5d ago)  /  8 (no vol confirmation)
Near 38W High ✅ KITE Daily Close (193 days) Close ≥ max(Close, 193d) × 0.92 — default (Kite depth ~38W)
38W High / Low ✅ KITE Daily High, Low (193 days) max(High, 193d) / min(Low, 193d) — from Kite history
Near 52W High ✅ NSE 52W High (NSE) Close ≥ wk52High × 0.92 — sourced from NSE weekHighLow.max. Badge shown in modal.
52W High / Low ✅ NSE NSE quote-equity weekHighLow 52W High = weekHighLow.max  /  52W Low = weekHighLow.min — official NSE 1-year range. 2,738 of 2,749 stocks have data. Primary target reference — replaces Kite 38W High.
Swing S/R Levels ✅ KITE CALC Daily High, Low (193 days) Local swing highs/lows confirmed by 5-bar window on each side → clustered within 1.5% → up to 3 resistance levels above price, 3 support levels below. Strength = number of swing points in cluster. Shown in stock modal.
ATH (9M) ✅ KITE Daily High (193 days) max(High, 193d) — Kite history depth. Default for now. ⚠ Decision pending: replace with true ATH when NSE data available.
MM Target ✅ KITE Daily High, Low (6 days)  ·  Current Price price + (max(High[−6:−1]) − min(Low[−6:−1]))
Target Price ✅ CALC MM Target  ·  52W High (NSE)  ·  ATH(9M)  ·  Current Price Nearest of MM Target, 52W High, ATH(9M) that is still above current price. Shown as [MM] / [52W] / [ATH(9M)] in scanner.
MM is only shown for BREAKOUT, PRE-CROSS, COILING stages — in POST-CROSS, PULLBACK, TRENDING, NONE the nearest ceiling is 52W High or ATH(9M) instead.

Liquidity

Volume-based metrics computed from Kite OHLCV. Avg 20 Vol baseline excludes last 5 days to avoid coiling period distortion.

MetricSourceRaw Data RequiredFormula
Avg 20 Vol ✅ KITE Daily Volume (25 days) Mean of days −25 to −5 (excludes most recent 5 days so coiling period does not distort baseline)
Daily Vol (₹ Cr) ✅ KITE Daily Volume (25 days)  ·  Current Price Avg 20 Vol × Price ÷ 10,000,000
Vol Ratio ✅ KITE Daily Volume (25 days) Today volume ÷ Avg 20 Vol
